# Weeknight Tacos

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef or turkey
- 1 packet taco seasoning (or homemade mix)
- Corn tortillas
- Toppings: onion, cilantro, lime, salsa

## Steps

1. Brown the meat, drain excess fat.
2. Add seasoning and a splash of water, simmer 5 minutes.
3. Warm tortillas, assemble tacos with toppings.
