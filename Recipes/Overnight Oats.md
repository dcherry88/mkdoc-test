# Overnight Oats

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk (or milk alternative)
- 1/4 cup yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- Honey and fruit to top

## Steps

1. Combine oats, milk, yogurt, and chia seeds in a jar.
2. Stir well, cover, refrigerate overnight.
3. Top with fruit and honey before eating.
