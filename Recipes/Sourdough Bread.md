# Sourdough Bread

## Ingredients

- 500g bread flour
- 350g water
- 100g active starter
- 10g salt

## Steps

1. Mix flour and water, autolyse 30 minutes.
2. Add starter and salt, mix well.
3. Bulk ferment 4-6 hours with folds every 30 minutes.
4. Shape and cold proof overnight.
5. Bake at 500°F in a Dutch oven, 20 min covered / 20 min uncovered.
